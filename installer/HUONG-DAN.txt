AUTO_VEO3 - HUONG DAN NHANH
============================

1. Giai nen toan bo thu muc nay vao noi ban muon (Desktop, D:\Tools...). Khong can quyen quan tri,
   khong can cai Python hay ffmpeg.
2. Mo Auto_veo3.exe.
3. Bam "Dang nhap bang Google". Trinh duyet mo ra: dang nhap D100Radar (neu chua) roi bam
   "Cho phep thiet bi nay". Quay lai app, vai giay sau app tu dang nhap.
4. Dan prompt (moi dong mot prompt, nen dung tieng Anh), chon ty le, thu muc luu, bam
   "BAT DAU TAO VIDEO". Ket qua tu tai ve thu muc da chon.
5. Het luot/het han: bam "MUA GOI CUOC", quet ma QR bang app ngan hang. He thong tu kich hoat.

LUU Y
- Windows 10/11 64-bit, can Internet.
- Neu Windows hien "Windows da bao ve PC cua ban": bam "Thong tin them" -> "Van chay".
- Doi may: vao https://d100radar.com/veo3/devices.html de go may cu.
- Du lieu (dang nhap, lich su) nam o %APPDATA%\Auto_veo3. Xoa thu muc app khong mat dang nhap.
- Can ho tro: bam nut "Ho tro" trong app, sao chep thong tin gui quan tri.
